Barangay Data Privacy Notice & Terms

Barangay Guadalupe Viejo, Makati City — Digital Resident Services Portal

1. Statement of Privacy Compliance

In compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), Barangay Guadalupe Viejo is committed to protecting your personal information. By logging in, you authorize the Barangay Administration to collect, process, and store your personal data solely for official government transactions, document processing, physical/digital Barangay ID issuance, and resident verification.

2. Information Collected & Processing Purpose

  • Full Name, Birthdate, Civil Status, Address, & Contact Details
  • Barangay Document Requests (Clearances, Indigency, Permits)
  • Resident Verification & Emergency Assistance Records

3. Confidentiality & Security Safeguards

Your personal data will not be shared with unauthorized third parties or commercial entities. All records are stored under strict barangay administrative oversight and secure access protocols.

4. Your Rights under RA 10173

You reserve the right to access, inspect, request corrections, or inquire about your personal records on file at Barangay Guadalupe Viejo, Makati City.
