ABOUT E-VIEJO

Bringing barangay services closer to every Guadalupe Viejo resident.

E-Viejo is the official digital resident services portal of Barangay Guadalupe Viejo, Makati City. Designed to make document clearances, resident IDs, blotter reporting, and administrative requests more convenient, transparent, and accessible under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).
